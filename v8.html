bio

Hemavarne is a physician and a postdoctoral research fellow at the Sadhguru Center for a Conscious Planet at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center and Harvard Medical School. She is curious about how simple yogic practices, offered through digital tools, might support the mental health of young adults. Hemavarne earned her medical degree from The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University and worked as a Residential Medical Officer at MGM Healthcare in Chennai, gaining broad clinical experience before choosing a path.
