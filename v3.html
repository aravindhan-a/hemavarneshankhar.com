Physician · Early-career researcher

Hi, I'm Hemavarne.

I'm a physician and a postdoctoral research fellow at the Sadhguru Center for a Conscious Planet at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center and Harvard Medical School in Boston.

I'm interested in how simple yogic practices, offered through digital tools, might support the mental health of young adults, and in what inner wellbeing could mean for everyday clinical care.

Before moving into research, I worked as a Residential Medical Officer at MGM Healthcare in Chennai, after earning my medical degree from The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University. My time on the wards is where many of my questions began.

I'm early in my research journey and always glad to learn from, and work with, others who share these questions.
