About Hema

Hemavarne earned her medical degree from The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University and worked as a Residential Medical Officer at MGM Healthcare in Chennai, gaining broad clinical experience before choosing a path.

On the wards she began to wonder whether yoga and consciousness could improve clinical outcomes and how patients experience their time in hospital. These practices had shaped her own growth as a physician, and she wanted to understand what they might offer others.

In 2025 she joined the Sadhguru Center for a Conscious Planet in Boston, where she helps study digital mind-body practices. Outside work, music and dance are a big part of her life.
