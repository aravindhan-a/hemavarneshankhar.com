Meet Dr. Hemavarne Shankhar

Physician & Early-Career Researcher

Hemavarne is a physician and a postdoctoral research fellow at the Sadhguru Center for a Conscious Planet at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center and Harvard Medical School. She is curious about how simple yogic practices, offered through digital tools, might support the mental health of young adults.

About

From the Wards to Research

Hemavarne earned her medical degree from The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University. Before specialising, she chose to gain broad clinical experience as a Residential Medical Officer at MGM Healthcare in Chennai.

On the wards she began to wonder whether yoga and consciousness could improve clinical outcomes and how patients experience their time in hospital. These practices had shaped her own growth as a physician, and she wanted to understand what they might offer others.

In 2025 she joined the Sadhguru Center for a Conscious Planet in Boston as a postdoctoral research fellow, where she continues to learn and contribute to research on digital mind-body practices.
